Walrasian Labor Market
================

Walrasian Equilibrium :

$$
L^s(w^*) = L^d(w^*) = L^*
$$

- $L^s(w^*)$ : Labor supply function (The workers)

- $L^d(w^*)$ : Labor demand function (The Firms)

The market clears when the quantity of labor demanded by the firms
($L^*$) exactly equals to the quantity of labor supplied by the workers
at a specific market-clearing wage ($w*$)

With :

$$
\frac{\delta L^s}{\delta w} < 0 , \frac{\delta L^d}{\delta w} > 0
$$

Wage $w*$ can be expressed as :
$w* = \frac{U'_{leisure}}{U'_{consumption}}$. This ratio is called
Marginal Rate of Substitution.
